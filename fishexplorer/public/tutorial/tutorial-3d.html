Visualization of 3D regions

  • Anatomical browsers that allow you to view and explore anatomical models from arbitrary angles are common today. Let’s say we want to display the Caudal hypothalamus in 3D. If you have already completed the tutorial “Visualize of 2D Regions”, just click the 3D viewer tab on the top. You will then see an image at the bottom of the page. If you started FishExplorer freshly, you are already in the 3D-viewer as shown in image below.

  • Now, imagine that you want to display, for example, the Caudal hypothalamus. To do this, search for the anatomical region by typing the first few letters in the white search box(encircled with a red line as shown below) in the list-viewer on the right. Alternatively, you can scroll through the list of regions. The list of regions matching the search criteria will appear on the screen as shown below.
  • Next, select the checkbox after the region name, e.g. Caudal hypothalamus (encircled with a green line). The three-dimensional surface is then displayed in the 3D viewer.
